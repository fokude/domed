# Roadmap

Planned, roughly in order.

- **Playtesting** with groups of real players, and fixing what it turns up
- **Bots** to fill near-empty servers until enough players are online
- **Cosmetics unlocked by level**: rail beam colors, kill effects, and weapon skins
- **More maps and artwork**
- **Mobile and controller support**

## Done

- Capture the Flag, Team Deathmatch, Domination and Hardpoint
- Themed maps and map voting
- Saved stats, XP and levels
- Hidden skill rating for fair teams
- Perks and kill feedback
- Test Range and the warm-up between matches
- Crosshair settings
